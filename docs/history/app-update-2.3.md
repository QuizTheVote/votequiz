## 🎯 **KEY FEATURES IMPLEMENTED**

### **Social Value Orientation (SVO) Framework**
- **Question Types:** agree_5, support_3, pick_1_3/4/5, binary_choice, multiple_choice
- **Psychological Foundation:** 40+ years of research-based approach
- **Competitive Advantage:** More sophisticated than simple policy-based quizzes

### **Flexible Template System**
- **Essential Questions:** 5 core questions for quick 2-3 minute quiz
- **Additional Questions:** 5 more for comprehensive 5-minute experience
- **Active Column:** Newsrooms can enable/disable questions as needed
- **Priority System:** Essential vs Additional categorization

### **Advanced Matching Algorithm**
- **Mixed Data Types:** Handles numeric scales and text responses
- **Jaccard Similarity:** For multiple choice questions
- **Topic Weighting:** User-defined importance ranking
- **Robust Parsing:** Handles various answer formats and edge cases

### **Professional Newsroom Tools**
- **Template Health Check:** Comprehensive validation with specific error reporting
- **Candidate Survey Generator:** Automated Google Form creation and response mapping
- **Publishing Guidance:** Step-by-step instructions with critical steps highlighted
- **Data Validation:** Built-in dropdowns and formatting to prevent common errors
